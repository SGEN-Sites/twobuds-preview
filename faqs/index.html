Visiting

5 questions
Where is Two Buds Dispensary?

696 East 241 Street, Bronx, NY 10470, in Wakefield. The closest subway is Wakefield-241st Street on the 2 train. Maps and more on our directions page.

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday, 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. Friday and Saturday, 10 a.m. to 11 p.m. Sundays and holidays, 11 a.m. to 9 p.m.

What do I need to bring?

A valid government photo ID showing you are 21 or older. In New York, adults 21+ may buy at licensed dispensaries with one.

Is Two Buds licensed?

Yes. Our license number is OCM-CAURD-24-000105. Licensed dispensaries in New York display the Office of Cannabis Management (OCM) dispensary verification QR code that shoppers can scan. What CAURD means.

Law & safety

4 questions
How much cannabis can I have?

Adults 21+ may possess up to 3 ounces of cannabis flower and up to 24 grams of concentrated cannabis. Rules can change; check the New York State Office of Cannabis Management (cannabis.ny.gov) for current law.

What are the limits on edibles?

In New York, edible products are limited to 10 mg THC per serving and 100 mg THC per package. Edibles often take 30 minutes to 2 hours to feel. Start low, go slow.

Where can I smoke?

Smoking and vaping cannabis follow the same rules as tobacco smoking, and New York City does not allow smoking in its parks, beaches, boardwalks and pedestrian plazas. It is illegal to drive impaired, and cannabis cannot be taken across state lines.

What if someone takes too much?

In case of accidental ingestion or overconsumption, contact the Poison Center at 1-800-222-1222 or call 9-1-1.
