The lineage

Sour Diesel is often said to descend from Chemdawg. Sources differ, but the Chemdawg name is on our menu too: Hepworth's 0.50g 510 Vape Cart · Chemdawg, Cru's 0.5g Infused PreRoll · Chemdawg and Honest Pharm's Chemdawg 1G Blunt.

On our menu

On the Nov 5 menu, 15 product names include Sour Diesel: 7 pre-rolls, 3 flower, 3 vapes and 2 concentrates. HERB's 1G PREROLL Sour Diesel made the menu's best-selling list. Flower comes as Smartbud's 1/2 oz Ground Flower Sour Diesel and Herb's 7G Ready To Roll Sour Diesel.

On the concentrate shelf, Pot & Head lists a 1g Cold Cured Rosin and a 1g Hash Cube in Sour Diesel. Vapes include Brass Knuckles' AIO Vape · East Coast Sour Diesel · 1g and Lost THC's New York Sour Diesel 0.5ml Live Resin Disposable.

Shopping tips

  • All 15 listings were tagged sativa on Nov 5; how it feels still varies from person to person
  • Deal check: on Nov 5 the Brass Knuckles AIO was in the BRASS KNUCKLES buy-one-get-one deal, and Pot & Head's concentrates were in Wax Wednesday
  • Ground flower and Ready To Roll are made for rolling your own
  • Concentrates are more potent than flower: start low, go slow
  • Ask at the counter to compare formats
