Gelato

Gelato spreads wide on our menu. It shows up in flower, carts, pre-rolls and more, often as one half of a cross.

See all 29 on the menuStrain library
TB·01 TB·01HybridGelato

The lineage

Gelato is often listed as Sunset Sherbet × Thin Mint Girl Scout Cookies. Sources differ on the details, so read any lineage as a family tree, not a promise.

Gelato is also a parent name in its own right. Runtz is often listed as Zkittlez × Gelato, and Lemon Cherry Gelato carries the name in its own. That's a lot of family for one word.

On our menu

On the Nov 5, 2026 menu, 29 product names include Gelato: 13 flower, 8 carts, 6 pre-rolls, a concentrate and an edible. Only some of them are Gelato itself, like Herb's 3.5g Gelato and Rove's Cartridge · Gelato · I · 1.0g. Gelato 41 appears too, on a Jaunty live resin cartridge and a 28 gram flower listing from Grocery.

The rest are crosses and spin-offs. Lemon Cherry Gelato alone accounts for 10 of the 29, and you'll also find Black Cherry Gelato from Butter and Cultured, Scarlet Gelato from The Plug Pack and Guava Gelato from Brass Knuckles. Tagged listings lean hybrid (16), with 6 indica and 2 sativa.

Shopping tips

  • Read the full name: Gelato in a title is often one half of a cross
  • Check the tag on each listing; Gelato names on our menu carry all three letters
  • Deal check: on Nov 5, Butter's and Cultured's Black Cherry Gelato were part of SPOOKY SPECIALS
  • New to a format? Start low, go slow
  • Ask at the counter which Gelato listing fits what you're after

Questions, answered

Is Gelato a hybrid?

Most Gelato-named listings on our Nov 5, 2026 menu were tagged hybrid (16), with 6 indica and 2 sativa. Many of those are crosses, so read the full name.

Where can I buy Gelato in the Bronx?

At Two Buds Dispensary, 696 East 241 Street in Wakefield, Bronx. Listings change, so check our menu for what's live, then order ahead or ask at the counter.
