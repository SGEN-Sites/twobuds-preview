Runtz

Runtz is a name that multiplies. On our menu you'll find it plain, doubled, rainbowed, chromed and even galactic.

The lineage

Runtz is often listed as Zkittlez × Gelato, which puts it in the Gelato family on paper. Sources differ, so read lineage as a family tree, not a forecast.

On our menu

On the Nov 5, 2026 menu, 20 product names include Runtz, and 10 of them are flower. Look for Herb's Flower · Runtz · 14g, Honey King's Indoor Flower 3.5G · Runtz and Wizard Trees' Rainbow Runtz 3.5G.

Variations stack up fast: Double Runtz, Rainbow Runtz, Chrome Runtz, Galactic Runtz and Pink Runtz, which comes as an NYCE Rosin Jar. Pre-rolls include Honest Pharm's Double Runtz 1G Blunt and Runtz's own 1.5g 2PK, because Runtz is also a brand on our menu, with 6 products of its own.

Tagged listings lean hybrid (12), with 5 indica and 3 sativa. Same name, three letters, so read the tag on the listing you pick.

Shopping tips

  • A name like Chrome Runtz or Pink Runtz is its own product: read the brand and the tag
  • Deal check: on Nov 5, Runtz listings sat in 5 EIGHTHS FOR $100, SPOOKY SPECIALS and Wax Wednesday
  • The NYCE Rosin Jar is a concentrate, more potent than flower: start low, go slow
  • Ask at the counter to compare
