Read the label first

Licensed products are lab-tested and labeled. Here's how to turn the numbers on a package, and on our menu, into a smarter pick.

THC by the numbers

Flower, pre-rolls, carts and concentrates show THC as a percentage. On our Nov 5, 2026 menu, most flower listings sat between 20% and 33% THC. Most carts sat between 71% and 92%, and most concentrates listed more than 50%.

A bigger number is a bigger number, not a promise of a better time. How a product feels varies from person to person, and cannabinoids and terpenes both play a part. New to a format, or to a number that high? Start low, go slow. Comparing two flowers? THC is one line of the story, not the headline.

Infused pre-rolls can run higher than flower. On the Nov 5 menu, most regular pre-rolls listed between 20% and 34% THC, and most infused pre-rolls listed between 27% and 48%.

CBD and the rest

Look for CBD next. On a lot of flower it reads zero: on Nov 5, 270 of the 313 flower listings that showed a CBD figure listed it at 0. If CBD matters to you, look for products that list it, then ask us.

Some products put the whole mix in the name. Ayrloom's AIO vapes, for example, list ratios like 600THC:150CBD:150CBG or 750THC:180CBD right on the menu. THC, CBD, CBG and CBN are all cannabinoids, and the balance between them is part of what you're choosing.

CBG and CBN turn up in other names too, like a GRON dark chocolate that lists CBN next to THC. When a name gives you a ratio, you're reading the recipe.

Check every package

  • Product type and size, like 3.5g of flower or a 1g cart
  • THC, and CBD when it's listed, as a percent or in milligrams
  • On edibles: THC per serving and per package
  • The classification if there is one: sativa, indica or hybrid
  • A batch or lot code. Keep the package until the product is finished
  • The brand, so you can find it again on our menu

Edibles count in milligrams

Edibles work in milligrams, not percentages. In New York, edible products are limited to 10 mg THC per serving and 100 mg THC per package, so the package tells you both numbers.

Edibles also take longer to feel, often 30 minutes to 2 hours, and they last longer than smoking. Read the serving size before the first bite. Our edibles guide goes deeper.

One more edible habit: the package is the label, so keep edibles in their original packaging, locked away from children and pets.

Size and price

Flower is sold by weight. Many flower listings on our menu show a size range such as 3.5g to 28g, and the price changes with the size you pick.

Pre-rolls and vapes put the size in the name instead: a 1g pre-roll, a 0.5g cart, a 2g all-in-one. Packs list the count and the total, like Hepworth's 0.50g 5pk [2.5g].

Menu prices are before tax. Deals change the math too, so check deals before checkout. On Nov 5, 2026 the menu ran 11 of them, from 5 EIGHTHS FOR $100 to the weekly Wax Wednesday deal on concentrates.

Check the shop too

A label only means something when it comes from a licensed source. Licensed dispensaries in New York display the Office of Cannabis Management (OCM) dispensary verification QR code that shoppers can scan. So scan it.

Our license number is OCM-CAURD-24-000105, and our line for all of this fits on a sticker: “Let's do it legit!” Or, the long version: “Experience the peace of mind that comes with purchasing from a trusted and transparent source.” Rules can change; check the New York State Office of Cannabis Management (cannabis.ny.gov) for current law.

Compare before you cart

Line up a few listings side by side on the menu: size, THC, CBD, brand, tag. Then pick.

Questions, answered

Is higher THC better?

Not automatically. A higher number means more THC, and how a product feels varies from person to person. Start low, go slow.

Why does my flower show 0 CBD?

Lots of flower on our menu lists CBD at zero. If CBD matters to you, look for products that list it, and ask us at the counter.

What does a size range like 3.5g to 28g mean?

The listing sells in more than one size, and the price changes with the size you choose.

Are menu prices before tax?

Yes. Prices on our menu are before tax.

How do I know a dispensary is licensed?

Licensed dispensaries in New York display the OCM dispensary verification QR code that shoppers can scan. Ours is licensed as OCM-CAURD-24-000105.
