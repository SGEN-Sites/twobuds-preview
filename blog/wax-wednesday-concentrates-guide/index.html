The deal

Wax Wednesday is our weekly concentrate deal, and its own words put it plainly: “Get 20% OFF all concentrates every Wednesday!”

It keeps going: “Stock up on your favorite wax, shatter, badder, and more while supplies last.” On the Nov 5, 2026 menu, the deal's list covered every one of our 54 concentrates. Not some. All of them.

Wednesday is the day, so plan around it. Browse early in the week, build a short list, and order when the deal is live.

What counts as a concentrate

Concentrates are cannabis products that are more potent than flower. Wax, shatter, badder, crumble, live resin and rosin all live in this family, and you'll see more names on the menu, like hash, kief, diamonds and sauce.

Our menu backs up the potency in numbers. On Nov 5, 2026, most flower listings showed between 20% and 33% THC, while most concentrate listings showed more than 50%. That gap is the reason to start low, go slow, even if you've smoked flower for years.

If you're coming from flower, the jump is real. Ask at the counter which format fits what you're used to, and how to start low with it.

On the shelf now

The Nov 5 shelf leaned hash, badder, diamonds, rosin and kief. A few names from that menu: Jetpacks' 1g Badder · Cherry AK47 and 1g Diamonds · Wedding Cake, NYCE's Rosin Jar · Pink Runtz · 1g, and Pot & Head's 1g Cold Cured Rosin · Sour Diesel.

Hash fans get International Hash in flavors like Garlic Naan and Cherry Cola, plus Hudson Cannabis' Hash · Sour Haze. For bigger sizes, Hepworth lists 3.5g Live Resin and 3.5g Sugar, and New York Honey lists 2g Dab Darts in Sour Tangie and The Original Z.

Sizes run from half-gram hash and a 0.5G badder to 2g Dab Darts and Hepworth's 3.5g listings, so read the size before you compare prices.

One more deal touched this shelf on Nov 5: HIGH FALLS SPECIAL, “20% OFF WHILE SUPPLIES LAST,” covering High Falls Canna's cured resins along with some of its flower and pre-rolls. Check deals for when each one ends, and ask us whether deals combine.

Handle with care

Concentrates are more potent than flower, so start low, go slow. How any product feels varies from person to person, and a new format is a fresh start even for a regular. Keep the deal in perspective, too: 20% off is a reason to try something, not a reason to overdo it.

Store them cool, dark, airtight and locked away from children and pets. Keep each one in its own container with its label, so you always know what's what.

Know the limit too: in New York, adults 21+ may possess up to 24 grams of concentrated cannabis. Rules can change; check the New York State Office of Cannabis Management (cannabis.ny.gov) for current law.

Questions, answered

Is Wax Wednesday every week?

The deal itself says “every Wednesday.” Check the live menu to confirm this week's run.

Does Wax Wednesday work for delivery?

The deal lists “Delivery available.” Delivery options and fees for your address show at checkout on our menu.

Which concentrates are included?

On the Nov 5, 2026 menu, the deal's list covered all 54 concentrates.

What's the possession limit for concentrates?

In New York, adults 21+ may possess up to 24 grams of concentrated cannabis.

Can I order concentrates for pickup?

Yes. Order on the menu, choose In store, Curbside or Express, and wait for the text confirming your order is ready.

How should I store concentrates?

Cool, dark, airtight and locked away from children and pets, in their labeled containers.

Are concentrates stronger than flower?

Concentrates are more potent than flower. On our Nov 5 menu, most concentrates listed more than 50% THC. Start low, go slow.
