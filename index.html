Our story

A community hub, a cultural oasis

Founded by a formerly incarcerated entrepreneur with Jamaican roots, Two Buds embodies the rich culture and tradition of Jamaica’s cannabis heritage.

About Us

In addition to our wide range of cannabis offerings, Two Buds is committed to promoting education and advocacy around cannabis legalization and social justice. We believe in giving back to our community and empowering individuals who have been impacted by the War on Drugs, which is why we actively support local initiatives and organizations that work towards cannabis reform and equity.
