About this product

Kushy Punch delivers the Strawberry Rozay Infused Pre roll, a 1G sativa experience that combines premium flower with signature infusion craftsmanship. This sativa pre roll energizes and uplifts, making it ideal for creative pursuits, social gatherings, or daytime enjoyment. Strawberry Rozay brings a luscious flavor profile bursting with sweet strawberry notes and fruity undertones, creating a smooth and satisfying smoke from start to finish. The infusion amplifies potency while preserving the strain's natural terpenes and vibrant characteristics. Expect euphoric, focused, and happy effects that keep you motivated and engaged throughout your day. Perfectly rolled for convenience, this pre roll offers consistent burns and rich flavor in every puff. Whether you're seeking inspiration or looking to enhance your mood, Kushy Punch's Strawberry Rozay delivers quality and reliability in a ready-to-enjoy format.
